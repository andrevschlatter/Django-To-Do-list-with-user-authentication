## How the tenure column was built

LinkedIn itself, Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo are all blocked from this environment, so no profile was scraped directly. The sample is search-tool snippets of indexed LinkedIn profiles, aggregator profiles (MarketScreener, ZoomInfo, RocketReach), Companies House appointment snippets, prospectus and team-page join years, and press on departures and fund launches. Sample sizes per firm: D1 17 names (0 dated leavers), Coatue 24 (8 dated leavers), Lone Pine 18 (6), TCI 17 (10), Pershing 21 (10), Soroban 9 (2), Valley Forge 2 (0), Himalaya 3 (3), Akre 8 (3), Fundsmith 13 (4).

Biases to keep in mind: press over-samples senior people who leave to launch funds, which inflates leaver tenure; junior quiet exits are nearly invisible, especially at D1 and Coatue; current-staff tenures are right-censored; aggregator dates are year-level and sometimes stale or conflicting; several key exits (Lone Pine's 2026 wave, Granat, Pershing's Feng, Fundsmith's Barnard) fall just outside 2016–2025.

## What changed and why

- Year-1 rows survive everywhere. Nudged up at D1, Coatue and nudged down at TCI, within the evidence.
- Ten-year rows were too low by three to five times at the four firms with real disclosure (Pershing, TCI, Akre, Fundsmith) for anyone who makes partner, and too high for the majority who leave earlier. Splitting "if partner / if not" fixes both errors.
- Tenure was wrong in both directions: Pershing and Fundsmith understated by about half; Himalaya's one visible seat turns over every 1–2 years; Coatue's figure is right for the junior track but not the firm.
- Leader pay cannot be subtracted from fees. Rich List figures include gains on own capital: Hohn $4.9B credited for 2025 against an $81.6M dividend; half of Ackman's 2020 $1.4B was his PSH shares. Where filed, the founder's cash take is a floor and the Rich List is a ceiling.
- No article, survey or filing reports analyst-level pay at any of the nine firms for any year, so every analyst figure is an affordability bound, a filed partner average, or a forum anecdote.
