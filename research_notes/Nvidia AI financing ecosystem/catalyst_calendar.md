**How to read the tags**
- [CONFIRMED DATE] = stated in a company filing/press release, or computed directly from an SEC rule applied to a filing-confirmed date (noted as "rule-derived").
- [ESTIMATED FROM PRIOR YEARS] = not announced; estimate based on last year's date taken from EDGAR filing dates.
- [ANONYMOUSLY SOURCED] = press report citing unnamed people.
- [UNVERIFIED] = single secondary source, aggregator, conflicting sources, or my own arithmetic on an unconfirmed base date.

**Method limits the report writer must know**
- Only `sec.gov` / `data.sec.gov` could be opened directly in this session. Every non-SEC item below was seen as a search-result summary only (the page itself could not be fetched: Nvidia IR/newsroom, CNBC, Reuters, Fed, Senate Banking, Oracle IR, etc. are outside the session's allowed network domains). Treat non-SEC wording as paraphrase, not verbatim quotes, unless shown in quotation marks from an SEC document.
- The shared web-search budget ran out after about 45 searches. Topics that were queued but never searched are listed in the Gaps of each section (Fed rate expectations, private-credit redemption windows, Section 232, CES keynote, rating-agency review dates, Stargate site milestones, AMD/Arista/Supermicro/Applied Digital/IREN announced dates, xAI/SoftBank deadlines).
- All SEC items were published in 2026; any source predating Nov 17, 2026 is marked "(pre-Nov 17)".

**Three corrections to the brief's background**
1. CoreWeave's ~$4.2B convertible and its ATM were launched Sept 17 and closed Sept 22, 2026, not Nov 10. Nov 10, 2026 was CoreWeave's Q3 earnings date. — [CoreWeave 8-K, SEC, filed 2026-09-22](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769628/000176962826000432/crwv-20260917.htm) (pre-Nov 17); [CoreWeave Q3 press release, SEC, 2026-11-10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769628/000176962826000461/coreweave3q26earningspress.htm) (pre-Nov 17)
2. The "up-to-$105B Nvidia-backed project" is Nvidia's residual value guaranties to SB Energy Corp. for the PORTS Technology Campus in Pike County, Ohio (about 4.25 GW leased to an OpenAI affiliate), signed Aug 17, 2026. It is a guarantee that becomes effective lease by lease, not a financing with a closing deadline. — [Nvidia 8-K, SEC, filed 2026-08-17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000069/nvda-20260817.htm) (pre-Nov 17)
3. The $500B "alliance" is six non-binding MOUs (Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, KKR) announced Aug 10–11, 2026 with no deployment timetable; I found no source that explicitly says "zero deployed", only that no terms, commitments or timetable were disclosed. — [Fortune, 2026-08-11](https://fortune.com/2026/08/11/nvidia-500-billion-ai-financing/) (pre-Nov 17); [DatacenterDynamics, Aug 2026](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/nvidia-partners-with-apollo-blackrock-blackstone-brookfield-goldman-sachs-and-kkr-for-500bn-financing-program/) (pre-Nov 17)
