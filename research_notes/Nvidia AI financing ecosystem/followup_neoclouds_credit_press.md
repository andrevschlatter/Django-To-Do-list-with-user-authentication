## 1. Private and newly listed neoclouds: who they are, how they are funded, Nvidia ties, IPO status

### Takeaway
Two filing-grade facts change the picture since the first pass: Anthropic's final prospectus (19 Nov) shows Lambda missed a lease deadline, so a $34.9B Anthropic contract is now terminable by either side; and SharonAI's 10-Q (16 Nov) describes the Nvidia rent-back structure in the issuer's own words. The IPO window is shut for the weaker names (Firmus pulled on 9 Oct; Nscale is reported to be weighing a delay to 2027), while ChronoScale and Cerebras, both already listed, still raised or guided up.

### Cited Findings

**ChronoScale (Nasdaq: CHRN): identified**
- ChronoScale Holdings Corp is the former Ekso Bionics Holdings (renamed May 2026). A business combination closed on 5 May 2026; the legacy Ekso business was sold on 30 Sep 2026. It describes itself as "an accelerated compute platform purpose-built to support demanding artificial intelligence workloads". `[FILING/ON-RECORD]` (pre-17 Nov) — [EDGAR filing index, CIK 1549084](https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0001549084.json); [ChronoScale fiscal Q1 2027 release, 15 Oct 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1549084/000149315226047278/ex99-1.htm)
- Fiscal Q1 2027 (quarter to 31 Aug 2026): revenue $41.5M (up 148%), net loss $26.4M, adjusted EBITDA $5.9M. Customers: a 50 MW Microsoft deployment "expected to integrate NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 systems", Baseten (about 4 MW), a Nutanix partnership. Management says contracts put it "on track for $1 billion in annualized run-rate revenue by calendar Q3 2027". It received $328.2M of customer prepayments. `[FILING/ON-RECORD]` (pre-17 Nov) — [release, 15 Oct 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1549084/000149315226047278/ex99-1.htm)
- The 18 Nov 2026 raise: "up to $750 million financing" = $250M of equity plus an up-to-$500M convertible preferred facility drawable over four years (initial minimum conversion price $12; only $10M drawn at closing). The common placement was priced at $8.00 a share, about 19.1M shares for $153.0M gross; part of the equity is "initially issued pursuant to a note structure held by Applied Digital Corporation". Named investors: "a leading frontier AI lab, Nutanix, Empery Digital Inc., and a storage infrastructure company". Goldman Sachs is lead placement agent. `[FILING/ON-RECORD]` (post) — [8-K, 18 Nov 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1549084/000149315226052998/form8-k.htm); [press release exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1549084/000149315226052998/ex99-1.htm)
- What the money is for: a 50/50 partnership (OD1 JV Holdings LLC) with STN One Inc. to build a data center in Odessa, Texas. The partnership takes over a GPU services agreement with "a leading frontier artificial intelligence lab" worth "up to $7.5 billion in total contract value... over approximately six years". First tranche due Q4 2026, the rest in 1H 2027. First phase is 65 MW with a right of first offer on 285 MW more. "A substantial portion of the first phase is backstopped by credit support from an investment-grade technology company". Blended revenue is "approximately $20.8 million of annual revenue per megawatt". `[FILING/ON-RECORD]` (post) — [8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1549084/000149315226052998/form8-k.htm); [release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1549084/000149315226052998/ex99-1.htm)
- Funding terms that matter for the credit story: (a) ChronoScale lends the partnership $220M, of which "$200 million... will be paid directly to Super Micro Computer, Inc. solely to fund the Partnership's purchase of GPUs". (b) Macquarie lends $74,687,500 for 90 days at a fixed 10%, interest paid in kind, secured on the GPUs, non-recourse to ChronoScale. (c) Project debt is not yet in place: STN's cash contribution is due only after "project financing for at least 75% of the project budget (a 'Qualified Project Financing')", and until then ChronoScale "is required to fund such additional amounts as are necessary to fund the Project, without a specified cap". (d) The customer gets a warrant for up to 8,072,152 ChronoScale shares at $0.01. (e) The customer may terminate affected tranches for "delayed delivery and sustained service level failures". No Nvidia guarantee or rent-back is mentioned. `[FILING/ON-RECORD]` (post) — [8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1549084/000149315226052998/form8-k.htm)

### Inferences
- Lambda's lease miss is the clearest filed example of the chain "no facility, no contract": 85% of its Anthropic commitment ($34.9B of $41.3B) is now cancellable at will by either side.
- Three listed or filing counterparties (Nscale, SharonAI, ChronoScale) all disclose the same gap: signed customer contracts, GPUs to buy, project debt not yet arranged. ChronoScale's partner contributes cash only if project financing for 75% of the budget closes.
- Filed pricing for small-borrower GPU debt in Oct-Nov 2026 is about 10% fixed (SharonAI 9.95%; ChronoScale/Macquarie 10% PIK for 90 days), not the "mid-to-high teens" in the Odd Lots account. The two may describe different borrowers or all-in costs.

### Gaps
- Lambda, Crusoe, Fluidstack, Firmus: no audited revenue, debt total or 2027 capex. All figures above are press or aggregator numbers.
- Together AI, Vultr, Voltage Park: no 2026 financial information found.
- Whether ChronoScale is still 96% owned by Applied Digital after the placement: not re-checked (the 13D/A of 20 Nov was not opened).
- Identity of ChronoScale's "leading frontier AI lab" customer, the "investment-grade technology company" backstop and STN One Inc.: not disclosed in the 8-K.

### Inferences
- The Information's $164.5B equals Nvidia's Q2 FY27 10-Q figures added together ($36B AI cloud agreements + $108.5B guarantees + $20B investment commitments). So the "$170bn" tally predates the Q3 numbers ($32B and $118.0B) and should not be mixed with them.
- If Moore's 25% is applied to Q3 revenue of $115.84B, neocloud-sourced revenue would be about $29B a quarter. That is my arithmetic on his share, not his figure, and "more coming from established players" means the financing-dependent part is smaller.
- CoreWeave's $6.3B is the only named dollar slice of the $32B found. With SharonAI's roughly $4B hardware programme and Nscale's and Firmus's unquantified deals, most of the $32B remains unattributed.
