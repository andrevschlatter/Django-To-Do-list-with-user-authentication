## 1. Verification of the five leads

### Takeaway
Three leads are confirmed in primary filings (Nvidia $36B to $32B with new conditions; Nebius $17-20B prepayments; CoreWeave $4.2B converts plus ATM, but both were September deals merely re-reported on 10 Nov). Two leads (LTV cut; Rowan quote) exist only in secondary write-ups I could not open, and the LTV story traces to a single podcast guest.

### Cited Findings
- **Nvidia neocloud commitments fell to $32B from $36B, with new conditions: CONFIRMED.** Q2 FY27 10-Q (as of 26 Jul 2026) shows "AI cloud agreements" of $36B (FY28 $6B, FY29 $8B, FY30 $7B, FY31 $6B, FY32+ $9B). `[FILING]` (pre-exit) — [Nvidia 10-Q, filed 26 Aug 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm)
- Q3 FY27 CFO commentary (as of 25 Oct 2026) shows "Neocloud agreements (formerly referred to as 'AI cloud agreements')" of $32B (FY28 $6B, FY29 $7B, FY30 $6B, FY31 $5B, FY32+ $8B). `[FILING]` (post) — [Nvidia CFO commentary, 8-K exhibit, 17 Nov 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000088/q3fy27cfocommentary.htm)
- New conditional language in the Q3 commentary, verbatim: "Our commitments take effect only if the neoclouds secure financing to purchase the data center infrastructure products and the related compute capacity is deployed, made operational, and accepted by us. The maximum commitment presented assumes these contingencies are satisfactorily resolved. If they are not timely resolved, the amount and timing of our commitments may be affected, and certain commitments could be reduced or eliminated." `[FILING]` (post) — [same](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000088/q3fy27cfocommentary.htm)

### Inferences
- The $4B drop in Nvidia's neocloud commitments sits alongside two possible causes the filing itself names: commitments "decrease as capacity is used by third-party customers" (Q2 wording) or are "reduced or eliminated" if financing contingencies are not met (Q3 wording). The filing does not say which drove the change. Do not report it as a cut caused by failed financings.
- The financing-contingent wording is new text in a document signed off after Huang left, but the commitments are measured at 25 Oct 2026, before he left.

### Gaps
- No primary source for the LTV numbers (no lender, rating agency or data provider). Treat as one market participant's account.
- No CNBC transcript for Rowan. Report as "reported by secondary outlets".
- Whether CoreWeave has sold any ATM shares since 30 Sep 2026 is unknown; no filing says.

## 2. CoreWeave (CRWV)

### Takeaway
CoreWeave is the largest and most leveraged neocloud: $47.5B of debt at 30 Sep 2026, $11.0B of principal due in 2027 and about $1.1B of interest a quarter, against $4.1B of cash. Its filed funding plan for growth leans on customer prepayments ($9B expected within three months), contract-backed DDTLs and vendor financing, all of which need open credit markets.

## 3. Nebius (NBIS)

### Takeaway
Nebius guides 2027 capex of $40-45B against 2027 revenue of $12-13B and says prepayments plus asset-backed financing on investment-grade contracts cover 75-85% of it. That leaves $6-11B a year to find elsewhere, and the plan depends on $17-20B of prepayments actually arriving.

### Inferences

## 10. Contagion chain: what the evidence supports

### Takeaway
Filings support each link of a chain from closed markets to cancelled capex to lower Nvidia shipments, but as stated contractual conditions and risk disclosures, not as events that have happened. I found no filed evidence of a cancelled Nvidia order, a triggered guarantee or a missed payment as of 22 Nov 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Link 1, growth capex depends on new external money: IREN's $6B "subject to market conditions"; Nebius's 15-25% uncovered plus dependence on $17-20B prepayments; Nscale's "reliance on uncommitted debt and equity financing"; Core Scientific's "additional external financing"; CoreWeave's $13.4B of principal due by end-2027. `[FILING]` — sections 2-5, 7
- Link 2, stated response is to cut capex: Nscale would "defer, reduce or cancel capital expenditure if such financing is not obtained". `[FILING]` — [Nscale S-1/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2110365/000119312526458722/ck0002110365-20261112.htm)
- Link 3, customer contracts are conditional on delivery and financing: Anthropic/Nscale depends on "qualifying financing"; Microsoft terminated a $3.22B Nscale statement of work after a power-connection failure; CoreWeave reclassified $1.9B of deferred revenue because "contracts became cancellable". `[FILING]` — [Nscale S-1/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2110365/000119312526458722/ck0002110365-20261112.htm); [CoreWeave 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769628/000176962826000465/crwv-20260930.htm)
- Link 4, Nvidia's support is itself conditional on the neocloud getting financed: "Our commitments take effect only if the neoclouds secure financing". `[FILING]` — [Nvidia Q3 CFO commentary](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000088/q3fy27cfocommentary.htm)
- Link 5, landlord exposure: CoreWeave is 84% of Core Scientific revenue and the sole Galaxy data center tenant; CoreWeave has $47.4B of leases not yet commenced plus a $16.9B capped lease. `[FILING]` — sections 2 and 7
- Link 6, OpenAI counterparty exposure: Nvidia's $105B guarantee is of OpenAI's lease obligations; OpenAI must find an A- rated replacement guarantor for the remaining 3.78 GW if Nvidia declines; CoreWeave has an OpenAI order form of up to $6.5B (other OpenAI contracts not restated in this 10-Q). `[FILING]` — [SB Energy S-1/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2133037/000162828026075026/sbenergy-sx1a3.htm); [CoreWeave 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769628/000176962826000465/crwv-20260930.htm)
- Delays already disclosed (all attributed to power, design changes or suppliers, none to financing): Nebius New Jersey tranches to 2Q27; IREN's $4B ARR target to January-February 2027; Nscale Loughton. `[FILING]` — sections 3-5
- Equity window after 17 Nov: ChronoScale's $8.00 common placement and up-to-$500M preferred on 18 Nov is the only post-17 Nov capital raise I found in filings. No neocloud filed an 8-K for a new debt or equity deal between 13 and 22 Nov. `[FILING]` — [ChronoScale 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1549084/000149315226052998/form8-k.htm)
- Market reaction to the exit (press): CoreWeave about minus 12-15%, Nebius minus 17%, IREN minus 8%; Nvidia's 4.5% 2031 bond at 5.717%; one headline claims "a third of market value wiped out" at Nvidia. `[SNIPPET-UNVERIFIED]` — [CNBC, 13 Nov 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-jensen-huang-resignation-ai-financing.html); [BigGo](https://finance.biggo.com/news/ab7ccc858d022c3e)

### Inferences
- Order of failure if both markets close, from the filed numbers: (a) uncommitted growth capex stops first (IREN's $6B, Nebius's uncovered share, Nscale's Monarch build); (b) Nvidia's conditional rent-backs then lapse by their own terms, removing the revenue floor that made GPU loans financeable; (c) CoreWeave, with $11B due in 2027, becomes a refinancing story rather than a growth story; (d) landlords with single-tenant exposure reprice; (e) Nvidia's "AI Clouds, Industrial & Enterprise" line (41% of revenue) slows. This ordering is my inference, not a sourced forecast.
- Offsetting evidence: prepayment shares are rising, IG-contract DDTLs priced tightly as late as 22 Oct, operating cash flow is large at CoreWeave ($5.6B in nine months), and Google is also acting as guarantor. The chain is conditional, not underway, on the filed record.
- The cause of Huang's exit is reported inconsistently across outlets and is outside this brief.

### Gaps
- No verified reports of stalled projects, lapsed guarantees, order push-outs or lender losses after 17 Nov. Absence in filings is weak evidence: 8-Ks lag and private lenders do not file.
- No analyst scenario for Nvidia revenue if neoclouds cannot fund.
