## 1. The Nov 20, 2026 "Oracle Statement": exact text and what it does and does not say

### Takeaway
The statement is real and the lead's paraphrase is accurate, but it left out the most unusual sentence: Oracle pre-emptively called *future* press reports wrong ("Any media reports that will come out and say the contrary are incorrect and wrong"). The statement gives no numbers for vendor financing, names no vendor, and defines "fully funded" only by reference to the Investor Day plan.

## 2. Investor Day (Oct 28, 2026): what Oracle presented for FY2027–FY2030, and the "$30 billion" question

The filed press release caps *additional capital-markets* raises through FY30 at $20B ($10B equity + $10B gross debt), excluding refinancing of existing debt and on top of the $20B ATM finished in Q1 FY27; vendor financing, customer-provided hardware and customer prepayments sit outside the cap. I found no Oracle statement of a "$30B still to raise" gap: the $30B figures in Oracle's own documents are the FY29 free-cash-flow target (and two unrelated items), so the user's recollection most likely conflates the FCF target with the funding need. ### Inferences
- The "$20B cap" announced Oct 28 is the second half of the "approximately $40 billion" FY27 raise Oracle had already flagged on June 10 ($20B ATM + $20B). What changed at Investor Day was (i) the promise that this is the last raise through FY30 and (ii) the debt half, which contradicts June's "does not expect to issue additional debt in calendar year 2026".
- The cap's stated exclusion is refinancing only. But the sentence that follows it means three other funding channels are outside the cap by construction: vendor financing, customer-provided hardware and customer prepayments. Leases (the $288B) are also outside it. So the cap limits Oracle-level bond and share sales, not total external financing of the build.
- On the user's "$30B it still needed": most likely a conflation with the FY29 FCF target. The only "still needed" amount Oracle stated was $20B, and it was raised within a week. I cannot rule out that a slide or Q&A answer quantified vendor financing or a residual need at ~$30B, because I could not read them.
