**Read this first (method and reliability).** Every WebFetch failed in this session: en.wikipedia.org, www.toledomuseum.org, www.theartnewspaper.com and bgindependentmedia.org all returned "host not resolvable / outside allowed network domains". So **no page was OPENED; every fact below is SNIPPET** (taken from a search-engine summary of the cited URL). "No link found" means the searches returned nothing, not that the museum certainly has nothing.

## 1. What the museum is known for, and what a child in the 1990s-2000s would have seen

### Takeaway
TMA was founded in 1901 by glass industrialist Edward Drummond Libbey, has free general admission, and is known for one of the world's most comprehensive glass collections, its 1933 Peristyle concert hall, medieval Cloister, and a strong European and American painting collection (Rubens, Van Gogh, Monet). The SANAA Glass Pavilion opened in August 2006, so a child of the 1990s would have seen the glass inside the main building; a child of the mid-2000s would have seen the new pavilion.

### Cited Findings
All SNIPPET.

**Founding and admission**
- Libbey and six others signed the articles of incorporation on 18 April 1901; the museum moved into its Greek-style building on Monroe Street in 1912. — [Holy Toledo History](https://www.holytoledohistory.com/post/125-years-of-art-for-all-the-toledo-museum-of-art-s-greatest-masterpiece); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toledo_Museum_of_Art)
- Libbey was president 1901-1925, funded construction, and bequeathed his Dutch and English art; Florence Scott Libbey is credited as co-founder. — [Frick directory](https://research.frick.org/directory/detail/1782/1); [BG Independent](https://bgindependentmedia.org/?p=79533)
- General admission is free; special exhibitions cost $10 for non-members. A 2006 Toledo Blade notice on the Glass Pavilion opening also said there was no admission fee. — [TMA visit page](https://toledomuseum.org/visit); [Toledo Blade 2006](https://toledoblade.com/Art/2006/08/24/Toledo-Museum-of-Art-celebrates-grand-opening-of-its-Glass-Pavilion.html)

**Glass and the Glass Pavilion**
- The glass holdings are "one of the most comprehensive and historically significant collections dedicated to the medium in the world"; Libbey's glassware and art collections were the basis of the museum's collection. — [Journal of Antiques](https://journalofantiques.com/columns/great-collections-april-2020/)
- Glass Pavilion: designed by Kazuyo Sejima and Ryue Nishizawa of SANAA (Tokyo, Pritzker Prize winners); joined the 36-acre campus in 2006 (grand opening covered by the Blade on 24 Aug 2006); interior and exterior walls are curved glass panels; cost about $30 million, much of it from the largest public fundraising drive in Toledo's history. — [BG Independent, 10th anniversary](https://bgindependentmedia.org/toledo-museum-marks-opening-of-glass-pavilion-with-hot-spot-exhibit/); [Toledo Blade 2006](https://toledoblade.com/Art/2006/08/24/Toledo-Museum-of-Art-celebrates-grand-opening-of-its-Glass-Pavilion.html)
- Free glassblowing demonstrations are offered daily at 2pm in the Glass Pavilion (current website). — [TMA visit page](https://toledomuseum.org/visit)

**Peristyle, Cloister, Classic Court, Gehry building**
- The Peristyle is a 1,750-seat neoclassical concert hall in the east wing, added in 1933, main home of the Toledo Symphony; riser seating is arranged in a half-circle like a Greek theatre. One source gives 1931. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toledo_Museum_of_Art); 1931 variant in [The Clio](https://theclio.com/entry/5188)
- The Cloister is built from real medieval French arcades: double capitals from the monastery of Saint-Pons-de-Thomières (late 11th to late 12th century; one capital shows the Weighing of Souls, the Damned in the Mouth of Hell, and Lust), plus arcade elements from the Cistercian abbey of Notre-Dame-de-Pontaut, and Cuxa capitals. — [TMA eMuseum, St-Pons capitals](https://emuseum.toledomuseum.org/objects/59324/double-capitals-from-saintponsdethomieres-weighing-of-so); [TMA eMuseum, Pontaut arcade](https://emuseum.toledomuseum.org/objects/59359/arcade-with-double-capitals-and-columns-possibly-from-notre); [TMA eMuseum, Cuxa/St-Pons photographs](https://emuseum.toledomuseum.org/objects/76147/toledo-museum-of-art-photographs-of-cloister-capitals--cuxa)
- The museum's bibliography for those objects cites a 1933 Art Bulletin article, "Medieval Cloister Arcades from St. Pons and Pontaut", and a January 1933 Museum News piece, "The Completed Museum Building". — [TMA eMuseum](https://emuseum.toledomuseum.org/objects/59324/double-capitals-from-saintponsdethomieres-weighing-of-so)
- The Classic Court shows ancient art, e.g. an Attic amphora by the Rycroft Painter with the Ransom of Hektor; the museum has "treasures from ancient Egypt, Greece and Rome". — [TMA Art Minute](https://toledomuseum.org/collection/art-minute/art-minute-rycroft-painter-amphora-with-the-ransom-of-hektor); [See Ohio First](https://seeohiofirst.org/sites/75)
- The Center for the Visual Arts next door was designed by Frank Gehry (snippet gives 1987; verify the year). — [Wikipedia mirror](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Toledo_Museum_of_Art)

**Best-known paintings**
- Rubens, *The Crowning of Saint Catherine*, bought from Albert Koppel in 1950; called "one of the jewels of our collection" by museum staff. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toledo_Museum_of_Art); [NEA Blue Star blog](https://www.arts.gov/stories/blog/2015/blue-star-museums-spotlight-toledo-museum-art)
- Van Gogh, *Wheat Fields with Reaper* (1888) and *Houses at Auvers* (1890). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toledo_Museum_of_Art)
- Monet, *Water Lilies* (1922), Libbey Endowment purchase. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toledo_Museum_of_Art)
- Other artists listed in the permanent collection: Holbein, Cole, Cropsey, Turner, Tissot, Degas, Cézanne, Matisse, Miró, Picasso, Calder, Bearden, Close, Kiefer; El Greco and Rembrandt are named (one source calls those "minor works"). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toledo_Museum_of_Art)
- Chuck Close, *Alex*: a portrait of the painter Alex Katz, enlarged by grid from a 24 x 20 inch Polaroid onto canvas. — [TMA eMuseum](https://emuseum.toledomuseum.org/objects/54766/alex)

**Frank Stella (died 2024) and big bold modern paintings**
- TMA owns three Stellas: *Lac Laronge IV* (1969, acrylic polymer on canvas), *Conway I* (1966, Irregular Polygons series) and *La penna di hu* (1987-2009). — [artdaily, 2013 purchase](https://artdaily.com/news/64101/Toledo-Museum-of-Art-purchases-two-American-abstract-works-of-art-by-Frank-Stella); [Tetragrammaton](https://www.tetragrammaton.com/art/laclaronge)
- *Conway I*: "a large horizontal rectangle into which a smaller parallelogram is inserted from below", in fluorescent alkyd and epoxy paints on canvas; shown in *Frank Stella: Irregular Polygons, 1965-66* at the Hood Museum and TMA (the Toledo run closed 24 July 2011). — [Toledo.com press release, 28 June 2013](https://tell.toledo.com/press-releases/2013/06/28/community-interest/toledo-museum-of-art-purchases-two-works-by-frank-stella/); [AFA News](https://afanews.com/item/212-irregular-polygons-by-american-master-frank-stella)
- *La penna di hu*: mixed media relief of etched magnesium, aluminum and fiberglass; credit line Libbey Endowment by exchange, 2012.104. In 2013 it hung in Gallery 1 and *Conway I* in the Wolfe Gallery for Contemporary Art, both in the east wing. — [Toledo.com press release](https://tell.toledo.com/press-releases/2013/06/28/community-interest/toledo-museum-of-art-purchases-two-works-by-frank-stella/)
- Stella spoke at the Peristyle in TMA's Masters Series (2011). — [artdaily](https://artdaily.com/news/46428/Frank-Stella-Appears-in-New-Masters-Series-at-Toledo-Museum-of-Art-Peristyle)

**Jewelry and decorative arts (the mother's craft)**
- Permanent collection includes ancient Greek gold, e.g. a ring in the form of a snake (acc. 1973.32), Hellenistic era. — [TMA eMuseum](https://emuseum.toledomuseum.org/objects/58764/ring-in-the-form-of-snake)
- *Radiance and Reverie: Jewels from the Collection of Neil Lane*: more than 150 historic pieces spanning a century of design, 18 Oct 2025 to 18 Jan 2026 (now closed). — [TMA exhibition page](https://toledomuseum.org/exhibitions/radiance-and-reverie-jewels-from-the-collection-of-neil-lane); [National Jeweler](https://nationaljeweler.com/articles/14327-neil-lane-jewelry-exhibition-coming-to-toledo-museum-of-art)
- Earlier show: *Radiant Ensemble: Jewelry from the Nancy & Gilbert Levine Collection*, European jewelry of 1785-1885 (date of show not found). — [artdaily](https://artdaily.com/news/28230/Radiant-Ensemble--Jewelry-from-the-Nancy---Gilbert-Levine-Collection-at-Toledo-Museum-of-Art)

### Inferences
- If the writer's childhood visits were before August 2006, he saw the glass collection in the main building, not the Glass Pavilion; a letter should not claim a childhood memory of the pavilion unless the dates fit.
- *Lac Laronge IV* was already in the collection before the 2013 purchases, so it is the Stella a child of the 1990s-2000s could plausibly have seen. Its acquisition year and whether it was on view then were not found.
- The Cloister most likely dates from the 1933 building completion (the bibliography points that way), but no snippet states the installation year outright.

### Gaps
- No colour or visual description of *Lac Laronge IV* was found, and no confirmation it is on view (most galleries are closed until fall 2027, see section 5).
- No specific El Greco or Matisse title at TMA was found. Note that a Matisse and a Cézanne may have been among the three paintings sold in 2022 (see section 2); not confirmed.
- No information on a studio or contemporary jewelry gallery, or on what the Classic Court looked like in the 1990s (mummies, etc.).
- No other large, bold, colourful modern painting on long-term view was identified beyond the Stellas.

## 2. Kerry James Marshall and other confirmed holdings by living artists on the list

### Takeaway
Confirmed TMA holdings (from the museum's own collection database or announcements): Kerry James Marshall, Mickalene Thomas, Amoako Boafo, Nina Chanel Abney, Julian Schnabel, Howardena Pindell, Sean Scully, Larry Poons, Nicole Eisenman (print), Alex Katz (print) and Jim Dine (print). Nothing was found for the rest.

### Cited Findings
All SNIPPET.

- **Kerry James Marshall**: *Portrait of a Black Man in a World of Trouble* (1990), acc. 2022.22, acquired 2022 with funds including the Jamar Art Fund of Marvin and Lenore Kobacker; described as acrylic on a burned American flag; the record calls it an early example of figurative paintings meant to "call attention to the absence of Black presence". Listed as not on view. — [TMA eMuseum](https://emuseum.toledomuseum.org/objects/81508/portrait-of-a-black-man-in-a-world-of-trouble); [TMA annual report](https://annualreport.toledomuseum.org/acquisitions); [BG Independent](https://bgindependentmedia.org/toledo-museum-of-art-broadens-collection-with-27-new-acquisitions/)
- Context: it was one of 27 works acquired in 2022, after TMA raised $51.2 million selling three permanent-collection paintings at Sotheby's in May 2022; The Art Newspaper framed it as swapping Impressionist paintings for a Marshall. — [The Art Newspaper](https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2022/08/31/acquisitions-tate-toledo-museum-ohio-indigenous-australian-art-museum-kerry-james-marshall); [artdaily](https://artdaily.com/news/148476/Toledo-Museum-of-Art-acquires-27-works-that-broaden-the-narrative-of-art-history)
- **Mickalene Thomas**: *Qusuquzah, Une Très Belle Négresse #4* (2023), rhinestones, silkscreen and acrylic on wood panel, 67 x 57 in., Libbey Endowment purchase; title refers to Manet's description of his model Laure. TMA made an "Art Minute" about it. — [TMA eMuseum](https://emuseum.toledomuseum.org/objects/82411/qusuquzah-une-tres-belle-negresse-4); [TMA Art Minute](https://toledomuseum.org/collection/art-minute/art-minute-mickalene-thomas-qusuquzah-une-tres-belle-negresse-4)
- **Amoako Boafo**: *White Picnic Blanket* (2021), large-scale painting, among the 200-plus works acquired in 2025; press release calls it "a provocative commentary on the disposition of the Black female body in the popular imagination"; said to be going on view in the newly installed Great Gallery; director Adam Levine reportedly visited Boafo's Accra studio. — [artdaily](https://artdaily.com/news/190897/Toledo-Museum-of-Art-acquires-over-200-artworks-in-2025); secondary aggregator [updown.ru](https://updown.ru/en/news/3911)
- **Nina Chanel Abney**: *Super Punk #198*, purchased 2024 (a digital work, bought shortly after a digital work by Yatreda). — [TMA news](https://toledomuseum.org/news/toledo-museum-of-art-acquires-over-250-artworks-in-2024)
- **Julian Schnabel**: *Portrait of a Freedom Fighter* (1984), 181.9 x 153 cm; record notes his broken-crockery paintings of the late 1970s and 1980s. — [TMA eMuseum](https://emuseum.toledomuseum.org/objects/60254/portrait-of-a-freedom-fighter)
- **Howardena Pindell**: *Art Crow / Jim Crow* (1988), accordion-fold artist's book reusing segregation-era photographs with the two phrases repeated across the pages; also *Untitled #32* (1974). — [TMA eMuseum](https://emuseum.toledomuseum.org/objects/42956)
- **Sean Scully**: *Ookbar* (1993-1994) and the 2018 print portfolio *Landlines and Robes*. — [TMA eMuseum paintings listing](http://emuseum.toledomuseum.org/categories/classifications/Paintings/images?page=58)
- **Larry Poons**: *Tantrum II* (1979) and *Monday Night Mark* (1965). — [TMA eMuseum paintings listing](http://emuseum.toledomuseum.org/categories/classifications/Paintings/images?page=58)
- **Nicole Eisenman**: lithograph *Sloppy Barroom Kiss*; the record notes she stopped painting in 2012 for eight months of printmaking. — [TMA eMuseum](https://emuseum.toledomuseum.org/objects/78163/sloppy-barroom-kiss)
- **Alex Katz**: print *Ada with Glasses* (1977); he is also the sitter in Chuck Close's painting *Alex*. — [TMA eMuseum works on paper](https://emuseum.toledomuseum.org/objects/images?filter=collections%3AWorks+on+Paper&page=10); [TMA eMuseum, *Alex*](https://emuseum.toledomuseum.org/objects/54766/alex)
- **Jim Dine**: print *Vermont (The Autumn)*, subject of a TMA "Art Minute"; other prints appear in the works-on-paper index. — [TMA eMuseum works on paper](https://emuseum.toledomuseum.org/objects/images?filter=collections%3AWorks+on+Paper&page=10)

### Inferences

## 5. Recent news, 2024-2026

### Takeaway
Most of the main building is closed for the first comprehensive reinstallation in over 40 years, with galleries reopening in fall 2027; Adam Levine appears still to be director. A visitor today cannot see most of the works named above.

### Cited Findings
All SNIPPET.
- Closures stay in place for the duration of the project, "with galleries reopening in fall 2027"; the Great Gallery and parts of the West Wing closed in December 2025. — [TMA, Reimagining Our Galleries](https://toledomuseum.org/reimagining-our-galleries); [BG Independent](https://bgindependentmedia.org/?p=112839)
- The new hang will run chronologically through about 6,000 years of art, returning to a loop layout; design partners are Michael Maltzan Architecture, Once-Future Office and Studio Zewde. Levine: "Our heating and cooling system is at the end of its usefulness" and "every gallery was going to have to be closed by 2027 anyway". — [Michael Maltzan Architecture](https://www.mmaltzan.com/?p=11386); [BG Independent](https://bgindependentmedia.org/?p=96747); [Toledo City Paper](https://toledocitypaper.com/?p=91571)
- Acquisitions: 264 works in 2024; over 200 in 2025 (including the Boafo). TMA also bought Park Seo-bo's *Ecriture No. 16-76* and hung it beside an Agnes Martin. — [TMA news](https://toledomuseum.org/news/toledo-museum-of-art-acquires-over-250-artworks-in-2024); [artdaily](https://artdaily.com/news/190897/Toledo-Museum-of-Art-acquires-over-200-artworks-in-2025); [Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10575109)
- Director: Adam Levine (Phillips interview; appointed to the University of Toledo board, Sept 2024). — [Phillips](https://www.phillips.com/article/69817815/adam-levine-on-whats-next-for-the-toledo-museum-of-art); [UToledo News](https://news.utoledo.edu/index.php/09_24_2024/art-museum-director-appointed-to-board-of-trustees)

### Inferences
- A letter should speak of the museum as the writer remembers it, and can mention that the galleries are being rehung for 2027; it should not say a given work "hangs" there now.

### Gaps
- No dated 2026 source naming Levine as director was found; searches showed no sign of a change.
- The snippet saying the Boafo "will be on view in the newly installed Great Gallery" conflicts in timing with the Great Gallery closing in December 2025; current display status is unknown.
