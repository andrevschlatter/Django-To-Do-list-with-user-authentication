# Market notes B: 25 living painters (as of 16 November 2026)

## How to read these notes

- **Every figure below is SNIPPET**: it comes from a search-result summary of the cited URL. **Nothing is OPENED.** All page fetches failed (heni.com, en.wikipedia.org and www.theartnewspaper.com did not resolve in this session), so no figure was checked against the page itself.
- Nothing is filled from memory. Where a search did not return a number, the note says "not found".
- **Source confidence.** Higher: The Art Newspaper, Artnet News, Culture Type, Apollo, auction-house lot pages, museum and gallery pages. Medium: HENI News (automated sale reports, consistent, premium-inclusive USD). Lower: aggregators (Appraisily, Art.Salon, Masterworks academy pages, VeryImportantLot, Invaluable). Appraisily "price range" maxima are repeatedly wrong (HKD read as USD) and are ignored unless stated.
- Prices include buyer's premium unless marked "hammer". Currency as reported.
- **Primary-market price ranges: not found for any of the 25 artists.** No source returned gallery prices. This is stated once here and not repeated per artist.
- Search budget: 61 of 70 searches used. Eddie Martinez received 4 searches (one over the 3-per-artist cap, by mistake); all others 3 or fewer.

## Cross-cutting observations (inference from the cited findings)

- **Rising:** Cecily Brown, Firelei Báez; Alvaro Barrington modestly, from a small base.
- **Cooling at auction, with documented resale losses:** Eddie Martinez (-59%), Robert Nava (-88% on one lot), Katherine Bernhardt (-80%), Oscar Murillo (-31%, -40%), Amoako Boafo (-26%), Katharina Grosse (-36%). Mickalene Thomas, Nina Chanel Abney and Tschabalala Self look soft on thinner evidence.

- **Unknown:** Genesis Tramaine (no 2025-26 data).
- Several artists whose auction prices have fallen have strong museum calendars (Grosse, Murillo, Thomas, Self, Adams). Auction weakness and institutional standing are moving separately for the 2019-22 cohort.
- HENI "negative return" articles select for losses on resale, so they may overstate weakness; they are single-lot comparisons, not indices.
