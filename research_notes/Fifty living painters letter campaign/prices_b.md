# Typical sale prices B: 25 living painters (as of 16 November 2026)

## Read this first

- **Nothing here is a published painting median.** No source returned a median for paintings alone for any of the 25 artists. Where an aggregator publishes a median, it covers all lots (prints, multiples, works on paper and paintings together) and is reported as such.
- **Every PAINTING_ESTIMATE is my own arithmetic** on the priced results listed in the same block (median where there are three or more results, mean where there are two). It is not a published figure. Records and obvious outliers are excluded and named.
- **All figures are SNIPPET-grade**: taken from search-result summaries or from the existing notes (market_notes_a.md, market_notes_b.md, group files), which were themselves snippet-based. I opened no page in this session. A few figures are marked "OPENED (earlier pass)": an earlier note-taker read those pages; I did not re-open them.
- Searches used: 25 of 25. Two went to Alex Katz and two to Jim Dine; none to Henry Taylor, Rashid Johnson or Oscar Murillo, whose existing notes already had priced results (so no print search was run for those three).
- **Currency conversion is approximate**, using rates implied by the sources' own conversions in the notes: £1 = $1.33, €1 = $1.17, HK$1 = $0.128. Converted figures are marked "≈".
- Prices include buyer's premium unless a source said otherwise; several aggregator figures do not say.
- Aggregators (Appraisily, Art.Salon, Masterworks, VeryImportantLot, Artpro, Invaluable, dealer pages) are lower confidence than auction-house pages, HENI News, Culture Type and The Art Newspaper.
- "Weak" means the estimate rests on fewer than three priced results.
