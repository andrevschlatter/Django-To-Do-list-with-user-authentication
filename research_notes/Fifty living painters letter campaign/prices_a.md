# Typical sale prices A: 25 living painters (as of 16 November 2026)

## Read this first

- **Nothing was opened in this session.** Every figure is SNIPPET: taken from a search-result summary or from the existing notes (market_notes_a.md, market_notes_b.md, group files). Where a source is tagged "OPENED (earlier pass)", an earlier pass in the group files read the page; I did not re-read it.
- Searches used: 25 of 25.
- **No source states a painting-only median for any of the 25 artists.** The only published medians are from the aggregator Appraisily and cover all media (prints and drawings included), so they sit far below painting prices. They are quoted under PAINTING_MEDIAN for reference and labelled as all-media.
- **PAINTING_ESTIMATE is my own arithmetic**, not a published figure: the median of the priced results listed on the same line (midpoint when there are two). Records and obvious outliers are left out. Prices include buyer's premium where the source says so; many aggregator figures do not say.
- Currency conversions are approximate: £1 = $1.30, €1 = $1.10, HK$7.8 = $1, CN¥7.25 = $1, C$1 = $0.72.
- "Agg." = aggregator (Appraisily, Art.Salon, Masterworks, VeryImportantLot, IntelArt, Vasari codex): lower confidence than auction-house pages, HENI, Culture Type or The Art Newspaper.
