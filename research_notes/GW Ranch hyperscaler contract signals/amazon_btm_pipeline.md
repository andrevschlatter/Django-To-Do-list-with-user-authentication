## Key Question 1: What onsite/BTM/co-located power deals has Amazon publicly signed so far (2024–2026), and what size?

### Takeaway
Amazon's confirmed onsite/co-located portfolio as of October 2026 consists of (1) the 7.65 GW-permitted, off-grid GW Ranch gas campus in Pecos County, Texas (confirmed by Amazon in August 2026; first power targeted H1 2027); (2) the Talen Susquehanna nuclear arrangement, restructured in June 2025 from a 960 MW co-located deal into a 1,920 MW front-of-the-meter PPA through 2042; (3) a 73 MW Bloom Energy fuel-cell array at Hilliard, Ohio, built by AEP Ohio at Amazon's cost; (4) utility-built "dedicated" gas generation for Amazon campuses in Indiana (2 x 1.3 GW + 400 MW BESS via NIPSCO GenCo) and temporary Duke Energy generators in North Carolina; (5) nuclear PPAs with Constellation (690 MW, Calvert Cliffs, 30 Sept 2026) and X-energy/Energy Northwest SMRs (320–960 MW, Washington) that are grid-delivered rather than BTM; and (6) an up-to-$8 billion Generac backup-generator supply deal (Sept 2026).

### Inferences
- Amazon's only true BTM/off-grid *prime power* project confirmed to date is GW Ranch; the Talen deal moved in the opposite direction (BTM → front-of-meter) after FERC's November 2024 rejection, and Calvert Cliffs was structured front-of-meter from the start. Amazon's preferred structures elsewhere are "utility-owned, customer-funded, dedicated" (Indiana GenCo, Hilliard via AEP Ohio, Duke temporary generators) rather than Amazon-owned generation.
- The GW Ranch turbine mix in the TCEQ technical review (21 x Siemens SGT-800 mid-size industrial units, 14 x Doosan H-class large-frame units, 2 x Wärtsilä 18V50SG engines) plus the separately announced Ansaldo 8 x AE64.3A order implies Pacifico is sourcing from non-GE Vernova/non-Mitsubishi OEMs with shorter backlogs; the 7.65 GW figure is nameplate across all permitted units, while "5 GW" is the delivered/nominal figure.

### Gaps
- Dollar value of the Amazon–Pacifico arrangement (land purchase price, PPA/tolling price, whether Amazon owns the plant or buys power) was not disclosed in any source found. Korben states "Amazon purchased the site and will buy the electricity produced," but this is a single secondary source.
- The exact Talen front-of-meter transition date (April 2026) is drawn from a search excerpt of the 10-Q; the filing text itself could not be retrieved.
- Whether the Hilliard fuel cells are now operating, and the ERAC appeal outcome, could not be determined.

## Key Question 2: What signals exist for unannounced Amazon BTM deals (permits, utility filings, supplier commentary, local news)?

### Takeaway
The strongest unannounced-deal signal is Homer City, Pennsylvania: in October 2026 Amazon Data Services filed a master plan for 36 data centers on ~1,100 acres to be powered by the 4.4–4.5 GW gas plant under construction there, while Amazon says only that it is "in discussions" with no definitive agreement.
