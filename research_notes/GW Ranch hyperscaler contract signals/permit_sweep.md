Every record below therefore rests on (a) agency document URLs surfaced by search, with details as quoted in search-engine extracts, or (b) secondary reporting of those records. ## Key Question 1: What do Texas databases (TCEQ, Texas Register/commissioners' agendas, PUCT/ERCOT, RRC, Comptroller JETI, county records) return for "GW Ranch", the named power companies, and Amazon/Microsoft affiliates?

### Takeaway
GW Ranch is fully documented in TCEQ records as Pacifico GW LLC's "GW Ranch Energy Center" (Permit 181033 / PSDTX1672 / GHGPSDTX255, filed Aug 4 2025, issued Jan 2026, 35 simple-cycle turbines, 7.65 GW ceiling), and Amazon's ownership was revealed not by any TCEQ filing but by three Aug 2026 TDLR building registrations by Amazon Data Services, Inc. at 6000 TX-18, Fort Stockton, plus a June 2026 Pecos County tax-abatement agenda for Pacifico GW LLC. The only other Texas record that directly ties a hyperscaler to a >1 GW onsite gas plant is Chevron's Energy Forge One LLC "Kilby" application (181895 / PSDTX1684 / GHGPSDTX260, Reeves County) paired with JETI J0022 and Microsoft's June 2026 20-year PPA. VoltaGrid is the only named power provider with TCEQ permits of its own (182467 Shackelford County; registration 177637 Bexar County); Liberty/LPI, Atlas/Moser, ProPetro/PROPWR and Solaris returned no Texas permit records under their own names.

### Inferences
- The TCEQ record for GW Ranch never names Amazon; the hyperscaler signal came from a different agency (TDLR) filed by Amazon Data Services, Inc. under its own name. The same TDLR-plus-satellite method is likely the fastest way to find the tenant behind other unnamed Texas gas-plant permits.
- The two confirmed hyperscaler BTM gas projects in West Texas (Amazon/GW Ranch in Pecos County; Microsoft/Kilby in Reeves County) both used the full NSR/PSD/GHG route with public notice, whereas VoltaGrid's Vantage campuses used minor-source standard-permit registrations. A >1 GW project filed through NSR/PSD will surface in TCEQ notices; sub-250 tpy modular engine fleets may not.
