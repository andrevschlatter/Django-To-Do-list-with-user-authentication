## Key Question 1: Who is developing GW Ranch, where exactly is it, and how big is it?

### Takeaway
GW Ranch is an off-grid ("private grid") gas-turbine power campus plus AI data center campus on roughly 8,000–8,400 acres along State Highway 18 about 17 miles north of Fort Stockton, Pecos County, Texas, developed by Pacifico Energy (Nate Franklin, founder/CEO) through the entity Pacifico GW LLC; it is air-permitted for 35 gas turbines (7.65 GW nameplate / 5,000 MW nominal) plus 1.8 GW of batteries and 750 MWac solar, with the land now owned by Amazon, which filed in August 2026 to build three ~189,000-sq-ft data center buildings there at ~$300M each.

## Key Question 2: What is the power supply plan and which equipment/power providers are involved?

### Takeaway
The air permit covers 35 simple-cycle gas turbines — 21 Siemens SGT-800s plus 14 large-frame H/J-class units whose vendor was undecided at permitting (Doosan DGT6-300H S2, GE 7HA.03, Mitsubishi M501JAC, or Siemens SGT6-9000HL were the modeled options) — with SCR/aqueous-ammonia NOx control, wet surface air coolers, 1.8 GW of batteries, 750 MWac solar, and gas delivered via multiple laterals including a 15-mile 1 Bcf/d pipeline to the Waha hub; the only confirmed vendor contract is Ansaldo Energia's July 2026 order from Pacifico for eight AE64.3A turbines for an unnamed "Texas data center project." None of the oilfield-power or midstream names in the research brief (Liberty, Atlas, ProPetro, Solaris, VoltaGrid, Kodiak, Energy Transfer, Williams, Kinder Morgan, Caterpillar, GE Vernova, Baker Hughes, Bloom) appears in connection with GW Ranch.

### Inferences
- GEM's Unit 1 (1,312.5 MW) ÷ 21 = 62.5 MW matches the SGT-800 class rating, and Unit 2 (6,342 MW) ÷ 14 ≈ 453 MW matches an H/J-class frame; GEM's two "units" almost certainly represent the two turbine groups in the permit.
- The Ansaldo AE64.3A order (eight ~78 MW units) does not match either permitted turbine class at GW Ranch (62.5 MW SGT-800 or ~453 MW frames), which suggests the Ansaldo units may be for Pacifico's other Texas project — or would require a permit amendment if deployed at GW Ranch. This is unconfirmed.
- GE Vernova appears only as one of four modeled large-frame options in the permit (7HA.03), not as a contracted supplier.

### Gaps
- Which vendor won the 14 large-frame turbines, and which turbines are "secured"/"on order," is not public.
- No gas supply agreement, pipeline operator, or shipper for the Waha lateral(s) was found; no Railroad Commission of Texas T-4 or pipeline permit tied to GW Ranch/Pacifico GW LLC surfaced.
- No battery (BESS) or solar EPC/supplier was found.
- The identity of Pacifico's second Texas data-center power project (referenced by POWER) was not determined; it may be the Brewster County site.

## Key Question 4: What permits and filings exist, with numbers and dates? Which are pending?
