## Key Question 1: Who are Solaris's and VoltaGrid's current data center customers, and what new contracts are expected or rumored? (fleet, suppliers, contracts, capex, earnings commentary)

### Takeaway
Solaris has three disclosed hyperscale/AI customers, none named by the company: the Stateline JV partner (an "affiliate of an industry leader in ... artificial intelligence computing", widely identified by third parties as xAI; ~900 MW), a "leading hyperscaler" (>500 MW, signed Feb 2026, starts Q1 2027) and an "affiliate of an investment grade, global technology company" (>600 MW, signed 24 Apr 2026, late 2026-2028 ramp), for >2 GW contracted on a 3,100 MW pro forma fleet; management says it is "in active negotiations for multiple gigawatts of additional demand with multiple hyperscalers and AI compute companies." VoltaGrid's disclosed customers are Oracle (2.3 GW, Oct 2025) and Vantage Data Centers (>1 GW, Feb 2025, including the ~700 MW Oracle/OpenAI "Frontier" site in Shackelford County, TX), on a ~7.5 GW order book through 2030; no Amazon or Microsoft contract, negotiation, or credible rumor was found for either company.

## Key Question 3: Does "GW Ranch" appear anywhere in connection with Solaris, VoltaGrid, or any other power provider on the list?

### Takeaway
"GW Ranch" is Pacifico Energy's 7.65 GW-permitted private-grid gas/solar/storage campus in Pecos County, Texas, now confirmed (Aug 2026) to have been acquired by Amazon, which says it will buy power from Pacifico; no source connects GW Ranch to Solaris, VoltaGrid, or any other provider on the list — the only equipment supplier ever named is GE Vernova (in a 2025 article about the pre-Amazon plan).

### Cited Findings
- GW Ranch is "a private-grid power generation campus located in West Texas, purpose-built for hyperscale data centers"; Pacifico says it can deliver "over 5GW of dedicated, private-grid power" combining gas, batteries and solar, with "99.999% reliability" — [Pacifico Energy, GW Ranch page](https://www.pacificoenergy.com/gw-ranch)
- 26 Jan 2026: Pacifico announced a TCEQ air permit for 7.65 GW of gas-fired generation, described as the largest power-generation air permit ever issued in the U.S.; first power scheduled Q1 2027; scalable to full capacity by ~2031 — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260126236053/en/Pacifico-Energy-Secures-7.65-GW-Power-Generation-Permit-for-GW-Ranch-Project); [Yahoo/BusinessWire](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/pacifico-energy-secures-7-65-120000106.html); [Chron](https://www.chron.com/news/science-environment/article/texas-data-center-power-21324591.php); [Turbomachinery](https://www.turbomachinerymag.com/view/pacifico-energy-obtains-air-permit-for-gw-ranch-project-in-texas)
