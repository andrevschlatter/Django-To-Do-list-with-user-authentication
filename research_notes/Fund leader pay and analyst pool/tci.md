## Key Question 1: What are the exact Companies House figures each year (turnover, operating profit, profit for the member with the largest entitlement, staff costs, headcount, LLP members) for TCI's UK entities, FY2016–FY2025?

### Takeaway
Press coverage of the UK filings gives a near-complete series for Hohn's dividend/pay (FY Feb 2017 ≈$364m; FY Feb 2018 $274m; FY Feb 2019 £200m/$278m; FY Feb 2020 $479m; FY Feb 2022 £575m/$690m; FY Feb 2023 £276m/$346m; FY Mar 2024 £42m/$53m; FY Mar 2025 £60m/$81.6m) and a few profit datapoints (PBT $1.06bn FY2022, ≈$200m FY2023; operating profit $456m → $229m/£182m into FY2024), but turnover, staff costs, average headcount and LLP member counts were not reported in any snippet I could retrieve, and FY Feb 2016 and FY Feb 2021 are unresolved.

### Inferences
- Hohn's UK-filed dividend series (USD, by FY end): Feb-16 ≈0; Feb-17 ≈$364m; Feb-18 $274m; Feb-19 $278m (£200m); Feb-20 $479m; Feb-21 ≈$152m (low confidence); Feb-22 $690m (£575m); Feb-23 $346m (£276m); Mar-24 $53m (£42m); Mar-25 $81.6m (£60m). Ten-year sum ≈ $2.7bn.
- The dividend is a poor proxy for the UK entity's profit, let alone the firm's: FY2023's $346m exceeded PBT of ≈$200m, while FY2024–25 dividends ($53m, $82m) were far below operating profit ($229m in FY2024; FY2025 revenue "surged"). The ≈£2.2bn rise in TCI Fund Management (UK) Ltd's net worth over 2020–2024 (with trivial cash) implies profits are being retained inside the UK company, most likely as an investment in the TCI fund itself. The retained profit still belongs economically to the shareholder (Hohn), so "Hohn's take" ≥ dividend.
- The move to a March year-end in 2024 and the appearance of "TCI Holdings Limited" as the consolidating entity suggest a group reorganisation around 2023–24; this coincides with the collapse in the headline dividend and the first disclosure of a 16-person equity-partner KMP group — consistent with partner economics now flowing through LLP profit shares rather than through the Ltd company dividend.
- Hedgeweek's "£233m pay cut" headline is simply £276m − £42m ≈ £234m (FY2023 → FY2024).

## Key Question 4: Fee structure, ownership, and how many investment professionals TCI employs; who are the known senior partners/PMs and what is known about their pay and tenure?
