# Empirical tenure sample: investment professionals at D1 Capital, Coatue and Lone Pine (2016–2026)

Method note (read first). Direct fetches of linkedin.com, google/bing/duckduckgo AND every press/aggregator host tried (hedgeweek, aol/Business Insider syndication, Institutional Investor, lonepinecapital.com, Wikipedia, The Information, The Block, Bloomberg, Yahoo, WSO, MarketScreener/Zonebourse, Apollo, ZoomInfo, Wiza, b17news, hedgeco, pionline) were blocked by the sandbox egress proxy. Every data point below therefore comes from WebSearch result snippets/summaries (~55 queries), not full-page reads. Dates are recorded only when a snippet stated them; anything I derived is labelled "inferred". Today's date is 2026-10-08, so "current tenure" = 2026 minus start year. Several of the most important Lone Pine departures occurred in 2026 (outside the 2016–2025 window requested) and are included because they are the "departure wave" the key question asks about.

## Key Question 1: Who are the named investment professionals at each firm, when did they join and leave, and where did they go?

### Takeaway
I assembled 18 Lone Pine names (12 with a sourced start year), 24 Coatue names (12 with start years, 8 with both start and end years), and 17 D1 names (only 3–6 with any sourced date and zero with a sourced departure). D1 is the thinnest because it was founded in July 2018, publishes no team page, and no press-reported investment-staff departure could be found; Lone Pine is the richest because its own team pages state "Joined LPC in YYYY".

## Key Question 2: What are the median and mean tenures of leavers vs. stayers at each firm, and how do they compare with the claimed averages (D1 3–5, Coatue 2–4, Lone Pine 4–6)?

### Takeaway
Among leavers with sourced dates, Lone Pine's median tenure is 8.5 yrs (n=6; 7.5 excluding the two 1998/2007 founder-era partners) and Coatue's is 4.75 yrs (n=8; 10 for public-equities seniors, 4.25 for venture GPs); D1 has no measurable leavers. Current-staff median tenure is ~5–6 yrs at Lone Pine, ~7.5 at Coatue, ~5 at D1. ## Key Question 3: Does Coatue run a structured 2–3-year analyst program with expected exits? What is the evidence?

### Takeaway
Yes for a new, explicitly structured junior track: in September 2025 Coatue announced an "AI Lab Junior Analyst Program" described as "a 2–3-year training track" for bankers with 0.5–3 years' experience; what happens at the end of the track is not stated. Older claims that Coatue's regular analyst seat is "two years and out" come only from anonymous, contested forum posts.

### Cited Findings
- Coatue's own post: "Coatue is launching a new AI Lab Junior Analyst Program, a 2–3-year training track designed to grow the next generation of analysts in a fully AI-native way. The ideal candidates are junior investment bankers with 0.5 - 3 years of experience..." (New York only; US work authorization required; nothing said about post-program outcomes) — [Coatue on X, Sep 2025](https://x.com/coatuemgmt/status/1970577298676908154).
