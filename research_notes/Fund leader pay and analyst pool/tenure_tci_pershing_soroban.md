# Empirical tenure sample: investment professionals at TCI Fund Management, Pershing Square Capital Management and Soroban Capital Partners

Research date: 8 October 2026. Method note: direct fetches of LinkedIn, Companies House, SEC EDGAR, Wikipedia, Institutional Investor, Hedgeweek, Fortune, MarketScreener, Tracxn, fintool, theorg and most press sites were blocked by the sandbox egress proxy, so every row below rests on search-engine-indexed snippets (LinkedIn headline snippets, MarketScreener/Zonebourse aggregator profiles, Companies House filing-history snippets, press and prospectus excerpts). Dates are year-level unless a month is stated; "inferred" rows are flagged. Tenure is computed to the stated end year, or to October 2026 for current staff. Roughly 50 search queries were run across the three firms.

## Key Question 1: Who are the named investment professionals at each firm, when did they join and leave, and where did they go?

### Takeaway
Across the three firms I could name 19 TCI, 20 Pershing Square and 13 Soroban investment-side individuals with at least a role and one date (fewer with both start and end dates). Pershing Square's current nine-person team is fully documented in its own filings; TCI's and Soroban's current rosters are only partially visible, and their departures are visible mainly when the leaver was a partner who launched a fund or when a trade-press item named them.

## Key Question 3: For Pershing Square, is the "no undesired departures" claim and the ~11-year average tenure of the current team consistent with the historical record of analyst departures 2010–2025?

### Takeaway
The ~11-year average tenure is arithmetically consistent with the current roster (mean 10.7–12.3 yrs depending on whether the 2026 hire and Ackman are counted), but it is a survivor statistic: between 2014 and 2025 at least seven named investment-team members left (Dinneen 2014, Hilal, Doyle and Rubin 2016, Namvar and Welch 2018, Feng 2025) plus one unnamed investment professional laid off in January 2018, out of a team that numbered roughly 8–10 at any time. Whether those were "undesired" is not independently verifiable; the firm's own phrasing in 2026 was that Ackman "couldn't think of any recent undesired departures."

### Inferences
- Consistency check on the ~11-year figure: my recomputation from stated join years gives a mean of 10.7 yrs for the seven non-Ackman members (12.3 yrs excluding the 2026 hire), so the firm's number is reproducible from public join dates.
- Consistency check on "no undesired departures": the record shows roughly 7–8 investment-team exits over 2014–2025 from a team of about nine, i.e. an annual turnover of roughly 6–8%, which would imply a steady-state mean tenure in the low-to-mid teens — compatible with a long-tenured core but not with "nobody leaves." Several exits (Dinneen after the Herbalife spotlight, Rubin after Valeant, the Jan 2018 layoff during redemptions) occurred at moments of stress, and press accounts at the time did not describe them as planned, so the "undesired" label is a management characterisation rather than something the public record can confirm or refute.
- The current team's long tenure reflects that no one hired 2009–2017 has left except Feng (2021 hire) and, before her, nobody hired after 2012 had left other than Doyle (a late-career adviser). The post-2017 hiring freeze (no new investment hires 2018–2020, one in 2021, one in 2026) also mechanically raises average tenure.
