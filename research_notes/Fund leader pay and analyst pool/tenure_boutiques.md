# Empirical tenure sample: investment professionals at Valley Forge Capital, Himalaya Capital, Akre Capital and Fundsmith LLP (2016–2025, with earlier context)

Method note: LinkedIn, Companies House, akrecapital.com, fundsmith.co.uk, Morningstar, Citywire, Investment Week, ZoomInfo, RocketReach and adviserinfo.sec.gov were all blocked for direct fetch by the sandbox proxy, so every data point below comes from search-engine snippets of those pages (via WebSearch) plus the few pages that were fetchable. Dates are therefore mostly year-level; "inferred" is flagged wherever a date is not stated by the source. Roles counted as "investment": analyst / research associate / research analyst / senior analyst / assistant PM / PM / partner-analyst / head of research. Excluded: founders (reported separately), IR/sales, operations, compliance, finance, legal, tech, HR, interns.

## Key Question 1: Who are the named investment professionals at each firm, when did they join and leave, and where did they go?

### Takeaway
Across the four firms I could name 25 non-founder investment professionals with at least a start year (Valley Forge 1–2, Himalaya 3, Akre 7, Fundsmith 12–13); 11 have left (Himalaya 3, Akre 4, Fundsmith 4–5). Destinations are mostly other buy-side firms or self-founded funds (Saberhagen → own fund; Himalaya alumni → Coatue, own funds; Patodia → JPMorgan AM; Morgan → Latitude IM; Diebold → Buffalo Funds), with two Akre 2014-vintage research associates leaving the investment track altogether.
